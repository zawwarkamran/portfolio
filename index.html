About

I'm a machine learning engineer and tech lead based in Toronto. For the past five-plus years I've built ML systems end to end: the data pipelines underneath, the models themselves, and the work of getting them into production and keeping them healthy.

Alongside that, I take on a small number of contract projects. I studied Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Toronto.
